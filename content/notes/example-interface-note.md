A short note should capture one useful idea quickly. For example: document interface assumptions explicitly when two signals look interchangeable but have different timing, trust, or ownership semantics.

The goal is not to turn every observation into an article. A few paragraphs, a code fragment, or a small diagram are enough when that is all the idea needs.
